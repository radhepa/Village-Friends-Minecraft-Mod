# Tall Tales Fishing: tall tales about the legendary fish, the season's fishing contest (day 12 of every season: fishing
# all day, weigh-in at the tavern at five, the biggest single fish wins), fishing chat, residents fishing at the dock,
# showing off a fresh catch, and the village fisherman selling the day's fish.
# Keys: tale.legend(.<personality>), tale.legend.fisherman, baby.tale.legend, tale.caught, contest.soon, contest.today,
# contest.won.player, contest.won.resident, fishing.chat, fishing.dock, fishing.catch, fishmonger.work.
# Placeholders: {legend} a legendary fish ("Old Whiskers", "the River King"), {legend_where} where it lives ("the deep sea",
# "the rivers and lakes of the plains"), {legend_when} when it bites ("on rainy nights", "whenever thunder rolls"),
# {contest_when} "today"/"tomorrow"/"in 3 days", {winner} a resident's first name, {catch} a fish in lower case ("pike").
# {legend}, {legend_where}, {legend_when}, {contest_when} and {catch} may begin with a small letter, so they never open a
# sentence and never follow "a" or "the". Tall tales name no time of day, weather or season of their own: {legend_when} does that.
