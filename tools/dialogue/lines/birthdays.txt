# Birthdays: the village calendar gives every resident a birthday (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter; 28 days each).
# On the day they wear a party hat, and everyone gathers at the bell in the evening for the party.
# Keys: greet.birthday(.close), baby.greet.birthday, birthday.thanks, birthday.gift(.loved), birthday.card, birthday.cake,
# birthday.soon, greet.party, chat.party, baby.party, greet.routine.party, routine.party, birthday.party.host, baby.birthday.*.
# Placeholders: {celebrant} whose birthday it is, {birthday} a date like "Summer 12", {when} "today"/"tomorrow"/"in 3 days", {season}.
# Party-routine lines (greet.routine.party, routine.party) never name the celebrant: the speaker may be the guest of honor.
