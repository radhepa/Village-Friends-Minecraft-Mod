# Notice board: notes residents pin up, in their own handwriting, and what they say once a notice is done.
# Keys: notice.hunt.<mob>, notice.fetch.<job> (all 25 jobs), notice.fetch.general, notice.fetch.child,
# notice.letter.<kind> (friend, family, crush, partner, apology), notice.birthday, notice.thanks.<kind>, baby.notice.thanks,
# letter.read.<kind> (the recipient opening a letter you carried).
# Placeholders: {mobs} "5 zombies" or "a witch", {wanted} "12 wheat" or "a cake", {recipient} and {sender} first names,
# {celebrant}, {birthday}, {when}. {mobs} and {wanted} are only ever objects ("deal with {mobs}"), never the subject of a verb,
# so they read right whether they're one or many.
