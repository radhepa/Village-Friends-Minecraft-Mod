# Homes: residents talking about the house and bed they call their own, and the notes they pin up when they need one.
# Keys: home.mine (they have a house and live there), home.shared (they share it with partner or family), home.homeless,
# home.crowded (the family is split or squeezed), home.new (just moved in), home.new.player (moved into a house the player
# built), home.newborn_bed (a baby in the family needs a bed), home.partner_moved (moved in with their partner),
# home.vacant (a neighbor's bed stands empty after a death), home.bedtime (heading home for the night), home.carried
# (woke in their own bed after a knockout), home.carried.cot (woke on the apothecary's cot), baby.home.mine, baby.home.new.
# Plaque readout flavour (one line under the readout): home.plaque.lived, home.plaque.empty, home.plaque.private.
# Notice board: notice.house (any household need), notice.house.homeless, notice.house.crowded, notice.house.newborn,
# notice.thanks.house, baby.notice.house.
# Placeholders: {house} a house name ("The Ashford House"), plus the usual {name}, {player}, {village}, {partner}.
# A line whose placeholder can't be filled is skipped, so every pool keeps lines that need no {house} or {partner}.
