# Offers: a resident notices something and offers to help. Each can come up once a day per resident
# when its conditions hold. Effects are carried out by TalkWorld.effect.

! patch_up_healer | when: hurt job:apothecary|job:cleric
Q: You're hurt! Sit down, let me see to that. I've got salve and clean bandages right here.
Q: Look at you, all scraped up. Hold still and I'll patch you.
- Yes, please :: Hold still... there. Clean, wrapped and on the mend. Try not to do that again. {heal} [HEART]
- I'm fine, really :: Fine? You're bleeding on my floor. All right, but come back if it gets worse. [SWEAT]

! patch_up_kind | when: hurt personality:warmhearted|personality:gentle|personality:protective
Q: Oh, you're hurt! Come here, let me look after you. I always keep bandages handy.
Q: You're limping, {player}. Let me wrap that for you. It won't take a moment.
- Thank you :: There we are. Gently, gently. You'll be right as rain soon. {heal} [HEART]
- It's just a scratch :: Just a scratch, they all say. Promise me you'll rest. [DOTS]

! patch_up_friend | when: hurt level:4
Q: You look rough. Let me patch you up before you go anywhere. That's what friends are for.
- Please :: Ow, I know, I know. Done. Now eat something and stay out of trouble for an hour. {heal} [NOTE]
- I'll manage :: You'll manage. You always do. But I worry, you know. [GLOOM]

! meal_trade | when: hungry job:cook|job:farmer|job:butcher|job:fisherman|job:tavern_keeper

Q: You look hungry, {player}. I've got food to spare. Want some?
- Yes, thank you :: Here. Eat it all, and properly, not on the run. {meal} [HEART]
- I've got food :: You've got food? Then eat it! Right now! I'm watching you. [ANGER]

! shelter_together | when: rain wet level:2

Q: Want to wait out the rain together? I'll tell you a story while we wait.
- I'd like that :: We watched the rain come down in silver sheets and talked about nothing in particular. It was lovely. {shelter} [HEART]
- I like the rain :: You like the rain? Off you go, then, you strange duck. Don't catch a cold. [NOTE]

! storm_shelter | when: thunder level:1

- I'll stay :: Thank you. Every flash is less scary with someone here. {shelter} [HEART]
- I'll be careful :: Careful isn't enough in a storm like this! ...Fine. Stay low. Move fast. [SWEAT]
